    Know the rules
    • Wetsuits are legal below 20°C, optional between 20 and 24.6°C, and banned above 24.6°C for standard and sprint distances. Below 11°C the swim cannot go ahead.
    • Wetsuit neoprene must be no thicker than 5mm.
    • Helmets must not cover or enclose your ears.
    • Age-group cyclists must keep 12 metres from the rider in front outside overtaking.
    • A small flannel is allowed in transition if it is kept inside your shoes or under the rack, never loose on the ground.
    • No riding before the mount line and no riding after the dismount line.
    • No personal audio devices during the race.

    Rules vary by event and distance under British Triathlon and World Triathlon. Always check your race's final race information.
